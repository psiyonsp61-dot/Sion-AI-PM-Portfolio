# AI PM Portfolio Context

## 1. Project purpose
This folder is a collection of product management learning assets, AI product strategy content, and hands-on PM exercises. It represents a structured learning journey for becoming an AI Product Manager (AI PM), combining classic PM foundations with modern AI/LLM product thinking.

The overall goal is not just to store course files, but to turn this knowledge into a strong personal portfolio that demonstrates:
- product thinking
- user research and problem framing
- PRD and roadmap planning
- research methodologies and data analysis
- product strategy and prioritization
- AI/LLM product understanding
- GTM, experimentation, metrics, and business modeling

This portfolio should show that you can think like a product leader who understands both business outcomes and AI-driven product experiences.

## 10. What to emphasize in writing
When writing case studies or portfolio pages, use a consistent structure:
- Problem
- User need
- Business context
- Approach
- Key insights
- Decisions made
- Metrics or success criteria
- Outcome / learnings

Example formula:
"Users struggled with X. I analyzed Y and identified Z. Based on market and user research, I recommended A. We prioritized B and C. Success would be measured by D, E, and F."

## 12. How to convert this folder into polished portfolio content
Use the learning files as raw material and convert them into sharpened portfolio outputs.

For each artifact, ask:
- What is the customer problem?
- What was the product opportunity?
- What were the key findings?
- What did I recommend?
- Why is this a good PM decision?
- What would I improve next?

This process turns training materials into professional portfolio work.
